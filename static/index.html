Reading your soil report

A few things to keep in mind.

pH

Indicates acidity or alkalinity. Crop preferences differ.

Nutrients

N, P and K results need their units and lab reference ranges for interpretation.

Next step

Discuss unusual values with a soil-testing laboratory or local agriculture officer before applying amendments.

Remember

Missing values are not zero. Only enter measurements that appear on your report.

KNOW YOUR WATER

Water quality

Store measured water-quality values to support better irrigation decisions.

Add water test report

Use measurements from a lab or test report.

ⓘ Water suitability depends on several parameters, crop, soil and irrigation method.

Why water quality matters

Water affects soil and crop performance.

pH

Helps describe acidity or alkalinity, but is not a complete water-quality test.

EC

Electrical conductivity can help indicate dissolved salts when interpreted with the correct units.

Expert check

Ask a qualified lab or agriculture specialist to interpret water quality for your crop and soil.
